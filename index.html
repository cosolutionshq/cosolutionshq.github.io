Straight answers.

Anything we didn't cover, ask us directly.

Ask on WhatsApp
We already use ClickUp, Monday or Excel. Why switch?

Those tools organise tasks. They don't tell you which client is profitable, how much capacity you have next month, or what revenue is coming. Your existing data comes with you at setup.

Will my team actually use it?

It's set up around your process, with your data, before anyone logs in. Each role is trained, and sees only what it needs.

Is it only for marketing agencies?

It's built for marketing agencies, digital studios and business consultancies: any team that sells its time on projects and retainers. The wording and setup adapt to how you work.

Where is my data, and who can see it?

On your own isolated install, with daily encrypted off-site backups. Inside the system, you decide who sees what.

Who can see salaries?

Only the people you allow. Access to money and salaries is set role by role and person by person.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes. Export everything to Excel or PDF at any time.

Is it in Arabic?

The interface is in English today. Your content, such as client names, notes and tasks, can be written in Arabic.

How long until we're live?

About four weeks from signing, depending on how quickly your data is ready.

What does it cost?

It's quoted after a short call, because setup depends on your size and structure. You pay one project fee plus an annual care fee. There is no per-seat subscription.
