Privacy

This website collects nothing about you. No forms, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

Updated 2 November 2026

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no forms, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts on this site, and it stores nothing in your browser.

What other services see

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page and keeps it in its logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. We receive none of this.

Everything else, including the typefaces and images, is served from this site. Nothing loads from any other company's servers.

The product screenshots

Every screenshot on this site shows sample data from a fictional agency. No real person's or client's data appears.

Contacting us

We use WhatsApp for contact. If you message us, we see your phone number, your WhatsApp name and what you write, and we use them only to reply to you. WhatsApp's own privacy policy covers the app itself.
